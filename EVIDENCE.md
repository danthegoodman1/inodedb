# Evidence

What checks each behavior `DESIGN.md` names: a test, a simulator property, or a planted bug in `scripts/mutants`. Keep it current as the design changes; a behavior without evidence goes under [Gaps](#gaps).

Notation:

- `ops::`, `groups::`, `keys::`, `leases::`, `snapshots::`, `feed::`, `standby::`, `posix::`, `preload::`, `seeds::` and `fsck::` are tests in `crates/sim/tests/<file>.rs`.
- `api::` is `tests/src/lib.rs` through `api_conformance!`, run on memory, SQLite, RocksDB and lionturtle, and on FoundationDB by `scripts/fdb`. `api-sb::` is `standby_conformance!`, on memory and FoundationDB.
- `sc::` is `crates/storage-conformance/src/lib.rs`, on every backend.
- `cli::` and `mount::` are `crates/cli/tests/*.rs`; `init_key::` is `crates/server/tests/init_key.rs`.
- `core/<module>::` and `server/<module>::` are unit tests in that crate's module; `transport::` is the transport crate's, `sqlite::` the SQLite backend's, `rocks::` the RocksDB backend's and `crates/storage-conformance/tests/rocksdb.rs`, and `lion::` the lionturtle backend's and `crates/storage-conformance/tests/lionturtle.rs`.
- P*n* is simulator property *n* (`DESIGN.md`, "Simulator"); M"…" is a planted bug.

## Simulator properties

| Property | Where `crates/sim/src/lib.rs` checks it | Reach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 Answers match the model | `check_commit`, `check_query` with `compare_answer`; ordering in `check_commit` and `answer` | Reads by path from writers and standbys, half of them through `answer_cached`; stats by inode, inside and outside the key's root |
| P2 Exactly once | `durable`, `reply`, `check_commit` | Retries, unknown outcomes, crashes, takeovers |
| P3 `when` and `all_or_nothing` | `Model::apply`, compared by `check_commit` and `durable` | `when_percent` up to 20, `atomic_percent` up to 30 |
| P4 Fencing | `landed` (no commit after a successor's); `answer` (nothing from a superseded writer's state) | Seeds with standbys and stalls |
| P5 `fsck` | `check_storage` after every commit; `fsck::check_with_log` at each run's end | Every seed |
| P6 Caches and snapshots | `probe`, `probe_snapshot` | Path reads; snapshots probed once caught up |
| P7 Release horizon | `lease_answer` | The consumer, under the tree's root or, on a share of its claims each seed draws (`scoped_percent`), under a directory it picks; each claim holds only entries that name its root |
| P8 Key scope | `check_query`; `durable` (a commit revokes exactly the keys whose roots it removed); `unrecorded` (each key's id is its minting version, and the writer's own commits delete only keys expired by their time); `answer` (refusals only for keys revoked, deleted, or expired on the fastest clock) | Write keys minted by the root, which half the seeds give TTLs of 20 ms to 3 s; stats by inode outside a key's root |
| P9 Truncation positions | `check_positions` against the simulator's record of live leases | The simulator truncates no log |
| P10 Listings of the free queue | `check_listing` | The consumer starts a listing on 30% of its turns, under the tree's root or a directory it picks, and asks for each page as the one before answers, from 1 to 20 entries a page. Under the root: files whose inode the simulator has seen, against the model. Under a directory: every entry any claim or listing has shown the consumer that names the directory |

Faults the simulator injects: clean shutdowns that release the store, unknown outcomes, storage latency and stalls, an old writer's reads from before its successor's commits, writer crashes mid-group, network delay, loss, duplication and reordering of client requests, client cut-offs, cut watch streams, and clock drift of up to 9% against a margin of 20% of the lease.

## Gaps

- The simulator never truncates the log, and lease, claim and listing calls bypass its network.
- P10 checks freed files alone, since the model keeps no replaced values; listings of replaced values have a scripted test only. The swarm checks a listing under a directory against the entries its consumer has seen, since the model keeps the directories above every name a file lost rather than those above its last; the model checks listings under the tree's root. Claims have no check that they leave nothing out.
- A store from before the queue kept keys under directories has scripted tests only: the swarm's stores begin with the record. A server from before those keys that writes to a store after a current one leaves entries under no directory, and nothing stops it.
- The pace of freeing under a busy writer, every 100 ms, has no test.
- The writer charges its groups for a store prefix of 16 bytes. A FoundationDB store with a longer prefix, a commit of lease heartbeats alone, whose budget counts keys and values, and a transaction through the storage trait itself can still pass FoundationDB's limit by its own count; the writer retries a commit that storage refuses as too large, and never splits it.
- The swarm's keys are write keys minted by the root, so read and admin grants, keys minted by keys, explicit revocation, and expiry's cascades and commit bounds have scripted tests only. The swarm checks that the writer deletes only expired keys, and a scripted test that it deletes them within a second; faults and takeovers would stretch any bound the swarm could check.
- A fenced or releasing writer's cached reads have scripted tests only: the swarm drops a stopped writer at once, and rarely reads during a release's commit.
- Changefeed progress changes and the "only committed versions" rule of `Watch` have no test.
- The client library has no unit tests; its cache restart after `Compacted`, heartbeat pacing and listing checks are covered only through `api::`.
- The snapshot miss path that waits for the feed never runs.
- `idb mount` on macOS is untested.
- The server's clocks across a sleeping host or a stepped wall clock, a check racing truncation, a claim cursor after an `Unavailable` claim, and redirect backoff have no test.
